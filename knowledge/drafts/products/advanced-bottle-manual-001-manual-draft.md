### DESCRIPTION OF THE HYDROGEN WATER GENERATOR

The hydrogen water generator is a household electrical device designed
to produce drinking water enriched with molecular hydrogen. The operation
is based on electrolysis: under the influence of an electric current, water
is enriched with molecular hydrogen.
• The package includes two mineral pouches. They enrich the
water with minerals and increase its pH level.
Composition: shungite, silicon, zeolite, magnesium.
The minerals do not remove organic contaminants. For deeper
purification, use an additional filtration system.
• The hydrogen water generator is equipped with UV-C LEDs
(~280 nm / ~450 nm) and an additional low-frequency functional
module (~7.8 Hz), which perform a supporting function and
enhance the overall operation of the device.
The device is designed for use with water that meets drinking
water standards.

04. Attention! Do not wash the main unit or lid under running water, and
do not immerse them partially or completely in water.

to avoid dry-heating damage.

### OPERATING RECOMMENDATIONS

+ If not used for a long time, fill the container with warm drinking water
(95-113°F /35-45°C) and leave for 30 minutes, then rinse before reuse.
+ Use only drinking water.
+ Drink hydrogen water immediately after preparation.
+ Do not use water above 113°F (45°C).
+ Do not leave water in the container for more than 4 hours.
+ Do not run electrolysis consecutively on the same cup of water
as pressure may build up.
