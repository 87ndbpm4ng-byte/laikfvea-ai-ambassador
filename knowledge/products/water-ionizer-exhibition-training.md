# Water Ionizer Exhibition Training Reference

This approved exhibition-training supplement applies only to `water-ionizer`. The official user manual remains the higher-priority source for operating and safety instructions.

## Four programs and electrolysis

The portable countertop Water Ionizer prepares Alkaline Water, Acidic Water, Hydrogen Water and Silver Ion without plumbing, drilling or permanent installation. A controlled direct current passes through suitable mineralized drinking water between a medical-grade stainless-steel cathode and a titanium anode coated with iridium/ruthenium oxides. A pressed-cotton membrane separates the fractions. The cathode fraction becomes more alkaline and typically has negative ORP; the anode fraction becomes more acidic and has positive ORP. pH and ORP are different measurements.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-TECHNOLOGY] HK2026 master training PDF, Water Ionizer sections 1, 5 and 6.

## Outputs and documented specifications

- Selectable pH: 2.5-11.2. Alkaline Water: pH 8.0-11.2, approximately -125 to -750 mV ORP. Acidic Water: pH 6.0-2.5, up to approximately +1000 mV ORP.
- Dedicated Hydrogen Water: up to 300 ppb H₂ / 0.30 ppm, approximately -200 mV; it does not intentionally change source-water pH. Prefer drinking it within about 20-30 minutes and do not heat it above 40°C / 104°F.
- Silver Ion: 0.01-20 mg/L Ag⁺, up to about 3.5 L; preparation can range from about 1 second to 200 minutes depending on selected concentration. It does not intentionally change source-water pH or ORP.
- Maximum batch: 3.5 L / 118.3 fl oz; internal cup 0.5 L / 16.9 fl oz. Dimensions: 29 × 14 × 25 cm; weight without water: up to 1.4 kg; input 110-230 V, 50-60 Hz; maximum ionization power 350 W.

Alkaline pH 8.0-9.4 is the documented everyday drinking/kitchen range; pH 9.5-11.2 should not be presented as standard everyday drinking water. Acidic-water applications beyond the approved exhibition wording require market-specific approval. Silver-Ion concentrations for regulatory-sensitive uses must not be independently selected or presented as medical/disinfection claims.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-OUTPUTS] HK2026 master training PDF, Water Ionizer sections 7-10, 14 and 19.

## Water, membrane and workflow

Start with suitable drinking water; the ionizer is not a purifier, reverse-osmosis system, desalination system or substitute for safe source water. Strongly demineralized RO water should be remineralized for conductivity. A Water Mineralizer is included for this relationship; it does not itself make unsafe water safe. For alkaline/acidic water, position the inner cup, fill it and the main pitcher, choose pH and start. For Hydrogen Water, remove the inner cup and select its program. For Silver Ion, remove the inner cup, install the removable silver electrode, select Ag⁺ concentration and start.

Before first normal use, rinse the pitcher and inner cup, run the instructed ionization cycle, and discard the first prepared alkaline/acidic batch. The included liquid test measures pH only; an ORP meter is not included. The device remembers the previously selected pH. The full model includes a display, progress/system messages, voice prompts, Simple/Full menu, quick programs and tilt protection.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-OPERATION] HK2026 master training PDF, Water Ionizer sections 15-18 and 21-24.

## Care, lifetime and boundaries

After use, wipe the cathode; mineral buildup may be addressed using the approved vinegar/citric-acid procedure. Do not abrade the coated anode. Remove, wipe and dry the silver electrode after use; darkening and normal silver-mass reduction are documented as normal rather than automatic faults. Do not dishwasher-clean the unit, submerge the electronic block, clean with water above 40°C, or connect power while descaling. White mineral sediment and a slight temperature increase during ionization can be normal consequences of source-water minerals and conductivity.

The training document lists a 36-month device warranty, 7-year cathode/anode warranty, 36-month silver-electrode warranty, anode service life up to 15,000 operating hours, and rated device service life up to 12 years when requirements are followed. It prohibits medical claims and says the device does not make contaminated water safe.
