## Initial setup and self-cleaning

1. Open the membrane's protective cap, drain the liquid, and dispose of the cap properly. Do not reuse it, as it is no longer needed.
2. Fill the bottle with clean drinking water.
3. Swipe left or right on the screen to switch modes. Tap the display to select "Self-Cleaning Mode."
4. Start the self-cleaning cycle.
5. After the cycle is complete, drain the water.
6. Repeat the cleaning process 3 times.

After completing all steps, the device is ready for use.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-HOW-TO-USE-21] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "HOW TO USE"

## Hydrogen water preparation process

1. Fill the generator with drinking water up to the 11.83 fl oz (350 ml) mark and close the lid.
2. To turn on the device, briefly press and hold the power button for about three seconds.
3. A touchscreen interface will appear. Choose the duration of hydrogen generation:
   - 3 minutes
   - 18 minutes
4. After selecting the mode, the electrolysis process will begin. The display will show a timer, hydrogen concentration (ppb) and ORP level (mV).
   - 3-minute mode — quick drinking mode.
   - 18-minute mode — deep hydrogen saturation and inhalation mode.
5. When the process is complete, the device will turn off automatically. Press the pressure release valve on the lid first, then open the lid.

Hydrogen concentration reaches 2000–9000 ppb, ORP down to −750 mV, depending on water quality.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-HYDROGEN-WATER-PREPARATION-PROCESS-22] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "HYDROGEN WATER PREPARATION PROCESS"

## Alkaline ionized water preparation with mineralization balls

1. Insert the mineral pouch (mineralization balls) into the cartridge and close the filter housing.
2. Fill the generator with drinking water up to 8.5–9.3 fl oz (250–275 mL).
3. Place the mineral cartridge into the generator bottle and close the lid.
4. Then follow steps 2–5 of the main instructions.

Recommended mode when using the mineral cartridge: 18 minutes.

After mineralization, remove the cartridge from the generator and dry it separately. Do not leave the cartridge in a humid environment inside the device.

Service life of the mineral pouch (2 pcs included): from 3 to 6 months, depending on usage intensity.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-ALKALINE-IONIZED-WATER-PREPARATION-WITH-MINERALIZATION-BALLS-23] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "ALKALINE IONIZED WATER PREPARATION (WITH MINERALIZATION BALLS)"

## Preparing the generator for hydrogen inhalation

To use the generator for hydrogen inhalation, you will need:

- The hydrogen water generator
- Inhalation adapter
- Hydrogen tube

1. Remove the generator lid.
2. Fill the container with 11.83 fl oz (350 ml) of drinking water.
3. Attach the inhalation adapter and insert the tube.
4. Select the 18-minute electrolysis mode.
5. Begin the process and start inhalation.

During inhalation, hydrogen is released through the tube, and the concentration shown may not reach maximum values — this is normal.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P1-PREPARING-THE-GENERATOR-FOR-HYDROGEN-INHALATION-01] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 1; original heading: "PREPARING THE GENERATOR FOR HYDROGEN INHALATION"

## Maintenance

1. Clean the bottle and lid using a slightly damp cloth moistened with water. Do not allow moisture to enter the charging port.
2. **Attention!** At least once a month, with regular use, it is necessary to clean the main unit. Fill the device with clean drinking water. Swipe left or right on the screen to switch modes. Swipe until "Self-Cleaning Mode" appears and start the cleaning cycle. After the cycle is complete, drain the water. Repeat the cleaning process if necessary.
3. **Attention!** Do not wash the body, main unit, or lid in a dishwasher.
4. **Attention!** Do not wash the main unit or lid under running water, and do not immerse them partially or completely in water.

If moisture enters the charging port, immediately disconnect the device from the power supply and do not use it until it is completely dry.

Ensure the moisture/maintenance cap is removed before operation to avoid dry-heating damage.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P1-MAINTENANCE-02] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 1; original heading: "MAINTENANCE"

## Charging the device

1. When the battery level drops below 10%, the screen will show a "Low Battery" warning.
2. Open the rear cover to access the Type-C charging port.
3. Use the supplied cable and adapter.
4. While charging, the device shows a charging indicator on the screen.
5. The device supports wireless charging. To charge, place the bottom part of the main unit onto a compatible wireless charging pad. The maximum wireless charging power is 5 W.

**Do not run hydrogen generation while charging.**

## Operating recommendations

- If not used for a long time, fill the container with warm drinking water (95–113°F / 35–45°C) and leave for 30 minutes, then rinse before reuse.
- Use only drinking water.
- Drink hydrogen water immediately after preparation.
- Do not use water above 113°F (45°C).
- Do not leave water in the container for more than 4 hours.
- Do not run electrolysis consecutively on the same cup of water, as pressure may build up.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-OPERATING-RECOMMENDATIONS-24] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "OPERATING RECOMMENDATIONS"

## How to open and close the lid

**To open the lid**

1. Press the pressure release valve to release pressure.
2. Press and lift the lid.

**To close the lid**

1. Align the lid with the opening.
2. Press and turn the lid clockwise until it is closed.

After the 18-minute mode, press the pressure release valve on the lid first to avoid resistance, and then open the lid.

Source: [ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-HOW-TO-OPEN-AND-CLOSE-THE-LID-25] ADVANCED-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "HOW TO OPEN AND CLOSE THE LID"

## Safety instructions

- Before using the generator, carefully read this user manual.
- Do not charge the device using fast-charging adapters. Use only the original charging device.
- Use only drinking water. Do not pour carbonated drinks, sweet beverages, tea, coffee, or any other liquids into the device.
- To avoid damage to the battery and control components, do not immerse the main unit or lid in water, and do not allow water to enter the charging port.
- Do not start the electrolysis cycle if the generator is not filled with water.
- Do not use water with a temperature above 113°F (45°C).
- Do not expose the device to temperatures below 32°F (0°C).
