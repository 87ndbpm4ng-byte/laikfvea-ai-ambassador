# Hydrogen Water Bottle GO Exhibition Training Reference

This supplement applies only to `everyday` (Hydrogen Water Bottle GO). Its official user manual remains higher priority for operation and safety.

## Purpose and hydrogen generation

GO is a simple portable hydrogen-water bottle: add suitable drinking water, start the one-button cycle, wait approximately five minutes and drink fresh. It uses electrolysis to dissolve molecular hydrogen in water. The documented result is up to approximately 1,500 ppb H₂ / 1.5 ppm and ORP down to approximately -650 mV, depending on source water, temperature, mineral composition, operating conditions, system condition and measurement method. Do not say every cycle always reaches 1,500 ppb.

Hydrogen Water means dissolved H₂; it is not automatically alkaline water. ORP is not a direct measure of hydrogen concentration. Hydrogen gradually escapes after generation, so the training source says to drink it soon after the cycle rather than heat or store it.

Source: [GO-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-TECHNOLOGY] HK2026 master training PDF, GO sections 1 and 4-9.

## Product details and use

GO has a 400 mL borosilicate-glass body, USB-C charging, one-button operation, built-in countdown timer, soft LED illumination, visible bubbles and an anti-slip base. The LED/bubbles make operation visible but are not evidence of hydrogen concentration. Use drinking-quality water only; it is not a purification system and cannot make unsafe water safe. Do not use juice, tea, coffee, carbonated or sweet drinks, milk, or other unapproved liquids.

Source: [GO-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-SPECS] HK2026 master training PDF, GO sections 7 and 10-15.

## Positioning and comparison boundaries

GO is for a simple, portable everyday Hydrogen Water routine at home, work, gym or while travelling. It is not a lesser PRO: GO prioritizes simplicity, a 400 mL capacity and an approximately five-minute routine. PRO has a 350 mL capacity and adds 3/18-minute modes, potentially higher documented H₂ concentration, inhalation, touchscreen, app functions, mineralization and wireless charging.

The Water Ionizer is a countertop, multi-water system: Hydrogen Water is one of four functions, up to approximately 300 ppb H₂ and up to approximately 3.5 L. GO is the portable personal bottle. Do not attach medical or therapeutic claims to Hydrogen Water or claim it is alkaline water.

Source: [GO-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-COMPARISON] HK2026 master training PDF, GO sections 16-20 and 29-30.

## Care and claim boundaries

Use the approved instructions for cleaning; do not use abrasive cleaners on the glass bottle or electronic base. The source asks staff not to turn source-water, concentration or lifestyle discussion into guaranteed health, disease, antioxidant, detoxification or treatment claims.

Source: [GO-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-CARE] HK2026 master training PDF, GO sections 22 and 28-30.
