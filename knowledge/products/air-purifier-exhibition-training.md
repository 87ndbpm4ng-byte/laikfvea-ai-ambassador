# Air Purifier Exhibition Training Reference

This approved exhibition-training supplement applies only to `air-purifier`. The official user manual remains the higher-priority operating and safety source.

## Technology and air path

The Air Purifier is a compact indoor photocatalytic system. Room air is drawn through a replaceable pre-filter and then a long-life glass photocatalytic element activated by 365 nm UV-A; treated air returns to the room. The glass element is described as a long-life/lifelong core component, not a routine disposable HEPA cartridge. The pre-filter captures larger dust and protects the internal chamber.

It is not a HEPA-based purifier: HEPA mechanically captures particles, while this product uses photocatalytic oxidation at an activated surface for susceptible organic and biological contaminants. Do not say HEPA is ineffective or that the product has no filters.

Source: [AIR-PURIFIER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-TECHNOLOGY] HK2026 master training PDF, Air Purifier sections 1, 4, 5 and 14.

## Documented specifications and modes

- Coverage: up to 20.25 m² / 218 sq ft.
- Modes: Night, Day and Boost.
- At 1 m: Night 29 dB(A) / 7 W; Day 31 dB(A) / 22 W; Boost 36 dB(A) / 27 W.
- Standby: approximately 5 W. Input: 110-240 V, 50-60 Hz.
- Dimensions: 17.7 × 25.75 cm. Weight: 1.8 kg / 3.9 lb. Power cord: approximately 177.8 cm / 70 in.
- The reference platform supports BLE 4.0, iOS/Android app control, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi for firmware updates, scheduling, pre-filter-life monitoring, RGB control, and a 180-minute automatic turn-off when no schedule is stored.

Night reduces fan speed, UV LED output and indicator brightness; Day is the balanced everyday mode; Boost uses maximum fan and UV output.

Source: [AIR-PURIFIER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-SPECS] HK2026 master training PDF, Air Purifier sections 1, 8 and 10.

## Maintenance, placement and normal behavior

Replace the pre-filter approximately every six months; the device/app can monitor its service interval. The glass photocatalytic element is not a routine consumable. Use the purifier indoors, vertically, on a stable table or raised surface, with air inlets and outlets unobstructed. Do not place it on the floor, near water, heavy steam, a heater or stove, or use a damaged cable. Avoid prolonged direct viewing of the UV LEDs through the upper diffuser.

After transport or storage below freezing, leave the device at positive room temperature for at least two hours and confirm that there is no condensation before switching on. A slight distinctive odor during the first hours after long inactivity is documented as normal: accumulated substances on the active surface can oxidize after startup. A red blink can indicate fan malfunction, violet a UV-module issue, blue that the pre-filter needs replacement; overheating can pause purification until the unit cools.

Source: [AIR-PURIFIER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-CARE] HK2026 master training PDF, Air Purifier sections 9, 11 and 12.

## Boundaries and claims

The product is not a medical device, room sterilizer, replacement for ventilation, humidifier/dehumidifier, professional mold remediation, high-capacity HEPA particle purifier, or guaranteed protection against infection. It is designed to act on susceptible VOCs, odors and biological contamination, but exact efficacy, virus, bacteria, mold, ozone and comparative-superiority percentages require the relevant approved report. Do not claim it removes every pollutant/allergen, completely eliminates mold, kills all viruses, or requires zero maintenance.
