# Air Humidifier Operating Instructions

This approved source applies only to the stable product identity `air-humidifier`. It is an ultrasonic household humidifier intended to raise and maintain indoor humidity by converting water into fine mist. The manual's wellness, sleep, respiratory, and skin language is not approved visitor-facing evidence and is excluded here.

## Product identity and package

The humidifier uses high-frequency vibration of an ultrasonic membrane to turn water into fine mist; a fan distributes the mist around the room. The manual identifies the main parts as the top assembly, steam separator, outlet nozzle, water tank, main unit, touch controls, air filter, float, piezoelectric element, and power cord. It supplies three air filters: one installed and two spares.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P5-P8-IDENTITY-01] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 5-8; "Air Humidifier Overview" and "Package Contents"

## Installation and first use

- Place the unit on a stable, level, firm surface. Do not place it on a tablecloth, carpet, or other surface that could block the underside air intake.
- Leave at least 1 m of clear space above the unit and at least 30 cm in front, behind, and to either side.
- Keep it away from direct sunlight, heat sources, and electronics that could be affected by condensation.
- Remove the steam-separator ring and separator before filling. Do not fill above the MAX line and do not pour water into the steam outlet.
- Fit the tank to the main unit with the front alignment marks matched. If they do not align, the unit stops after its fault indication.
- Connect the power cord to the main unit, then connect it to mains power.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P9-P11-SETUP-02] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 9-11; "Preparing for Use"

## Water and essential oils

Use fresh water. The manual describes distilled water, reverse-osmosis water, or tap water with mineral content up to 1000 mg/L as suitable and says not to use mineral water or water above 40°C.

Only 100% natural essential oils may be used. Fill one third of the water first, add the oil, then top up the water tank. Do not use oils containing synthetic fragrances, impurities, or solid substances, and keep oil off the outer device surfaces.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P3-P4-P10-WATER-03] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 3-4 and 10; safety and filling instructions

## Controls and operating modes

Press the on/off control to begin in the minimum steam setting. The steam-mode control cycles through four settings:

- Low: approximately 100 mL/h.
- Medium: approximately 200 mL/h.
- High: approximately 300 mL/h.
- Night: approximately 35 mL/h.

The timer control offers 2, 4, or 6 hours. When no timer is selected, the manual documents automatic shutoff eight hours after switching on. The backlight control provides steady or flicker lighting in Low, Medium, and High modes; Night mode provides candle lighting.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P12-P13-CONTROLS-04] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 12-13; "Controls and Indicators"

## Draining and storage

Before draining, switch the unit off, unplug it from mains power, and disconnect the power cable from the unit. Remove the upper assembly and tank, then drain the tank and main unit in the marked drain direction. Do not allow water into the air outlet. For storage or transport, fully drain and dry the tank and main unit separately; storing the unit wet or with water inside can allow bacterial or mold growth.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P14-P15-DRAINING-05] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 14-15; "Draining Water from the Unit"

## Cleaning and maintenance

With regular use, clean and maintain the unit at least monthly. Disconnect power and drain the main unit before cleaning.

- Clean removable top parts and the tank with a soft cloth using diluted neutral detergent or soapy water when needed, then rinse and dry.
- Wipe the main unit and tank with a soft cloth. Do not apply strong force to the piezoelectric element; clean it gently with a damp cotton swab and warm water.
- For removable white scale, the manual permits a solution of 20 g citric acid in 200 mL warm water.
- Rinse the air filter with water, dry it thoroughly, and replace it if worn. Vacuum dust from the intake cover and slots.
- Do not use thinners, gasoline, alcohol, abrasive powder, chlorine, or hypochlorite-based cleaners.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P16-MAINTENANCE-06] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; page 16; "Maintenance / Care"

## Troubleshooting

- If the unit does not start, check power connection, water in the tank, and that the tank is correctly aligned with the main unit.
- For a leak, disconnect from mains power immediately. The manual identifies overfilling, water entering the steam duct, and debris under the water-supply valve as possible causes.
- For weak or absent steam, check for water in the steam duct, a dirty piezoelectric element, blocked air intake, dirty filter, or strong draft.
- The fan may run for 4-6 seconds after switching off to clear residual steam; the manual identifies this as normal.
- If the unit stops, beeps ten times, and the power button flashes ten times, the manual identifies a fan fault and directs the visitor to service support.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P17-P18-TROUBLESHOOTING-07] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 17-18; "Troubleshooting"

## Safety restrictions

- Do not disassemble or repair the unit yourself and do not use it with a damaged unit, cord, plug, or connector.
- Keep plugs and connectors dry. Disconnect power before cleaning.
- Use only with 220 V, 50 Hz AC power as specified by this manual.
- Do not block the steam outlet or put foreign objects inside it.
- Do not operate near a heater, in direct sunlight, near electronic devices, or on an unstable surface.
- Do not move, tilt, refill, or remove the tank while the unit is connected or operating.
- Do not wash the main unit in a dishwasher, under running water, or by immersion.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001-P3-P4-SAFETY-08] AIR-HUMIDIFIER-MANUAL-001; pages 3-4; "Safety Requirements"
