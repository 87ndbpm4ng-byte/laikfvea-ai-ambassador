# Air Humidifier Exhibition Training Reference

This supplement applies only to `air-humidifier`. Its official operating instructions remain the higher-priority source for operation and safety.

## Technology and specification

The Air Humidifier is a 3-in-1 ultrasonic humidifier, aroma diffuser and atmospheric light. A piezoelectric ceramic atomizer uses high-frequency vibration to create fine water droplets; it does not boil water or create hot steam. The top outlet releases the mist to add moisture to indoor air.

- Tank: 2.4 L. Maximum mist: 300 mL/h. Four modes: Night 35 mL/h, Low 100 mL/h, Medium 200 mL/h, High 300 mL/h.
- Recommended coverage: up to 36-38 m² / approximately 90 m³; actual coverage depends on room, ventilation, starting humidity, temperature, mode and open doors/windows.
- Timer: 2/4/6 hours. Automatic shut-off when water runs out. Noise: up to 38 dB. Rated voltage 220 V; rated power 25 W.
- Dimensions 170 × 170 × 300 mm; weight 990 g; 1.5 m cord; ABS + PP housing; atomizer expected life greater than 5,000 operating hours.

## Features, oils and maintenance boundaries

Only 100% natural essential oils are documented. Add water to about one third of the reservoir, add oil, then fill the rest; do not add concentrated oil to an empty reservoir unless separately instructed. Lighting includes constant light, flickering brightness and Candle mode in Night mode. The package lists three air filters (one installed and two spare).

The atomizer-hour figure is not a guaranteed number of years because water quality, mineral buildup, cleaning and use affect life. The master source says the exact Silver-Ion concentration for humidifier use still requires confirmation: do not independently choose a concentration or claim it sterilizes the room, kills airborne viruses, disinfects surfaces or prevents infection.

Source: [AIR-HUMIDIFIER-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-FEATURES] HK2026 master training PDF, Air Humidifier sections 15-18 and 23-27.

## Product isolation and claims

An Air Humidifier adds moisture; an Air Purifier treats certain contaminants passing through its purification system. They complement but do not replace one another. The humidifier is not an air purifier. It can be described as helping create a comfortable indoor humidity environment; do not turn that into disease, treatment, allergy, virus, sterilization or guaranteed health claims.
