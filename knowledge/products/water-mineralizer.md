# Water Mineralizer User Manual

This approved source applies only to the stable product identity `water-mineralizer`. The source manual calls the product **Severyanka mineral additive for water**; the exhibition-facing identity remains **Water Mineralizer**. It is a standalone concentrated liquid additive and is not the Advanced/PRO bottle's mineralisation cartridge or mineralisation balls.

## Product identity and documented purpose

The product is a concentrated mineral additive intended to add documented mineral ions to purified drinking water, particularly water that has undergone deep purification such as reverse osmosis. The manual says such treatment can leave water with mineralisation below 50 mg/L. This describes the manual's background and is not a guaranteed input or final value.

Source: [WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001-P1-PURPOSE-01] WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001; page 1; "Purpose"

## Composition

The concentrate contains purified water and ions of calcium, magnesium, and potassium in these documented concentrations:

| Component | Manual concentration |
| --- | --- |
| Calcium ions (Ca2+) | 20-30 g/L |
| Magnesium ions (Mg2+) | 10-15 g/L |
| Potassium ions (K+) | 6-8 g/L |

The source does not document the final concentration of each mineral after dilution or the final total dissolved mineral content of prepared water.

Source: [WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001-P1-COMPOSITION-02] WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001; page 1; "Composition"

## Preparation and use

Add the concentrate to purified and/or demineralized water. The recommended dilution is **1:1500**.

- For an Akvalife jug, use one measuring spoon (**2 mL**); the manual says the measuring spoon is included.
- Without the measuring spoon, use approximately **1/2 teaspoon** when using a normal kitchen spoon.
- For ordinary drinking water, use approximately **1/2 teaspoon per 3 L of water**.
- Mix the water thoroughly after adding the concentrate.

Prepared water may be used for drinking, cooking, and preparing beverages. When ordinary drinking water is used, the manual recommends taking its starting mineralisation into account. It allows a slight adjustment to the amount according to starting mineralisation and taste preference, and says small deviations from the recommended dosage do not affect the safety of using the water.

Source: [WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001-P1-P2-USE-03] WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001; pages 1-2; "Method of use"

## Storage and shelf life

- Store unopened in closed packaging at **-30 degrees C to +30 degrees C**.
- After opening, refrigerate at **2-8 degrees C**.
- Protect from direct sunlight.
- A small amount of sediment is allowed; shake thoroughly before use.
- Shelf life is **12 months from the date of manufacture**.

The manual does not state a separate after-opening use-by period.

Source: [WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001-P2-STORAGE-04] WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001; page 2; "Storage conditions" and "Shelf life"

## Safety restrictions

- Use only in the recommended proportion and dilute it in water before use.
- Do not consume the concentrate undiluted.
- Keep out of reach of children.

No charging, electrical power, filter, replaceable cartridge, cleaning procedure, device service life, preparation cycle, or troubleshooting process is documented because the source describes a liquid mineral additive rather than an electrical appliance.

Source: [WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001-P1-P2-SAFETY-05] WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001; pages 1-2; composition note and "Precautions"
