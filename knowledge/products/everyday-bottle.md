# Hydrogen Water Bottle GO User Manual

This approved document was reviewed directly against both supplied manual images. The exhibition-facing product name is **Hydrogen Water Bottle GO** and its stable internal product identity is `everyday`.

## Product identity and purpose

The manual names the product "Laikfvea Hydrogen Water Generator Go." It describes the product as a household electric device designed to produce drinking water enriched with molecular hydrogen during operation.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-DESCRIPTION-01] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "DESCRIPTION OF THE HYDROGEN WATER GENERATOR"

## Principle of operation

The manual states that operation is based on electrolysis: an electric current passes through the water, molecular bonds are broken, and hydrogen gas is produced.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-DESCRIPTION-02] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "DESCRIPTION OF THE HYDROGEN WATER GENERATOR"

## Package contents

1. Hydrogen water generator
2. USB cable

4. User manual

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-PACKAGE-CONTENTS-03] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "PACKAGE CONTENTS"

## Main components and charging-port location

The main components shown in the manual are:

1. Pressure release valve
2. Lid
3. Body
4. Main unit

The Type-C charging port is located on the side of the base. Connect the charging cable securely to ensure proper charging.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-STRUCTURE-04] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "STRUCTURE OF THE HYDROGEN WATER GENERATOR"

## General use and charging restriction

The generator starts working after the power button is pressed. A small amount of water may remain inside; the manual states that this is normal.

**Do not run hydrogen generation while charging.**

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-HOW-TO-USE-05] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "HOW TO USE"

## First use

Before first use:

1. Fill the container with clean drinking water at 95–113°F (35–45°C).
2. Let it sit for 20–30 minutes.
3. Empty the container.
4. Refill it with fresh water before each use.

The recommended amount of water is 10 fl oz (300 mL).

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-HYDROGEN-WATER-PREPARATION-06] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "HYDROGEN WATER PREPARATION PROCESS"

## Hydrogen water preparation process

1. Press the power button. The indicator lights up and the generator starts producing hydrogen water. Active bubble formation inside the container is normal.
2. The process duration is about 5 minutes.
3. When the process finishes, the generator turns off automatically.
4. Press the pressure release valve on the lid first, then open the lid.
5. The hydrogen-enriched water can then be consumed.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-HYDROGEN-WATER-PREPARATION-07] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "HYDROGEN WATER PREPARATION PROCESS"

## Operating recommendations

- Use only drinking water. Do not use carbonated, sweetened, or flavored drinks.
- The operating-recommendations section says the water temperature should not exceed 113°F (45°C).
- Drink the hydrogen water immediately after preparation.
- Do not leave water in the generator for more than 4 hours.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P2-OPERATING-RECOMMENDATIONS-08] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 2; original heading: "OPERATING RECOMMENDATIONS"

## Cleaning and maintenance

- Wipe the main unit with a soft dry cloth for cleaning.
- Do not submerge the generator in water or rinse it under running water.
- Do not wash the lid, body, or main unit in a dishwasher.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P1-SAFETY-INSTRUCTIONS-09] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 1; original heading: "SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS"

## Safety instructions

- Read the manual carefully before using the generator.
- Use only the supplied charging cable. Do not attempt to charge with incompatible adapters.
- Use only drinking water. Do not fill the generator with carbonated or flavored liquids.
- Do not submerge the generator in water or rinse it under running water.
- Do not open the housing while the generator is operating.
- Do not use the device at ambient temperatures above 113°F (45°C) or below 32°F (0°C).
- Do not leave the device unattended for long periods.
- Do not wash the lid, body, or main unit in a dishwasher.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-P1-SAFETY-INSTRUCTIONS-10] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; page 1; original heading: "SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS"

## Conservative temperature guidance

The technical-specifications table states a water temperature of up to 176°F (80°C), but the operating-recommendations section says water should not exceed 113°F (45°C), and first-use preparation specifies 95–113°F (35–45°C). Because these values conflict, visitor-facing operating guidance must use the conservative maximum of 113°F (45°C) until the manufacturer clarifies the 176°F (80°C) specification.

Source: [GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001-REVIEW-TEMPERATURE-12] GO-BOTTLE-MANUAL-001; pages 1–2; reviewed conflict between "TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS," "HYDROGEN WATER PREPARATION PROCESS," and "OPERATING RECOMMENDATIONS"
