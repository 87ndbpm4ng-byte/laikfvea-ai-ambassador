# Hydrogen Water Generator for Face & Body User Manual

This source applies only to the stable product identity `face-body-generator`. The Russian manual calls the device a portable hydrogen skin humidifier. The exhibition-facing identity is **Hydrogen Water Generator for Face & Body**.

## Product purpose and operating principle

The device is a portable skin-care water sprayer. The manual states that it converts water into a fine mist by ultrasound and saturates that mist with molecular hydrogen. Health, cosmetic, physiological, and therapeutic effects stated elsewhere in the manual are not approved knowledge.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P1-DESCRIPTION-01] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 1; panel "Description"

## Package contents and components

The package contains the portable device, a USB cable, and the user manual. Labeled parts are the lid, water reservoir, water-spray lens/nozzle, switch key, and main body.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P1-PACKAGE-02] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 1; panel "Package contents"

## Preparation before first use

Before first use, fill the reservoir with warm drinking water at 35-45°C, leave the device switched off for 30 minutes, drain the water, and rinse the reservoir several times under running water. Fully charge the device before use.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P2-PREPARATION-03] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 2; panel "Preparation for use"

## Charging

Connect the supplied USB cable's Type-C connector to the charging port, then connect its USB end to a power adapter. Charging from 0 to 100% takes 1-2 hours. The indicator is red during charging and turns green when fully charged. The manual recommends a 2A/5V USB adapter, prohibits fast charging, and says to use only the supplied cable. Keep the port and cable dry.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P2-CHARGING-04] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 2; panels "Preparation for use", "Operating recommendations", and "Safety instructions"

## Operating procedure

1. Unscrew the top lid counterclockwise.
2. Fill the reservoir with low- or medium-mineral drinking water and close the lid.
3. Slide the switch down to start spraying. The indicator turns blue.
4. One spray cycle lasts 55 seconds.
5. Do not tilt the device more than 45 degrees during use, to prevent water leakage and damage to the spray nozzle.
6. Slide the switch up to stop spraying.

Do not cover the spray lens/nozzle while the device is operating. The manual gives no recommended number of cycles per day or other usage frequency.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P2-OPERATION-05] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 2; panels "How to use" and "Operating recommendations"

## Water and storage requirements

Use only low- or medium-mineral drinking water. Do not put highly mineralized water, lotions, or cosmetic products into the device. Do not operate with an empty reservoir or use water above 45°C. If the product will not be used for a long time, drain the remaining water, do not tighten the lid fully, and refill with drinking water before the next use. Transport with water in the reservoir only in a vertical position. Do not expose the device to subzero temperatures.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P2-WATER-06] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 2; panels "Operating recommendations" and "Safety instructions"

## Cleaning and maintenance

Rinse the reservoir and lid with tap water. If the spray nozzle becomes dirty, rinse it with clean water. At least once a month, clean the reservoir with a solution of 3 g citric acid in 30 mL warm drinking water at 35-45°C: mix until dissolved, fill the reservoir, leave the switched-off device for 30 minutes, drain, and rinse the reservoir several times under running water. Do not wash the device in a dishwasher.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P2-MAINTENANCE-07] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 2; panel "Maintenance"

## Safety and restrictions

- Read the manual before use.
- Use only the supplied charging cable; the manual recommends a 2A/5V adapter and prohibits fast charging.
- Do not fill with highly mineralized water, lotions, or cosmetics.
- Do not immerse the device in water.
- Do not operate with an empty reservoir or with water above 45°C.
- Do not expose the device to subzero temperatures.
- Transport it with water only while vertical.
- Do not tilt it more than 45 degrees during use.
- If irritation or another adverse reaction occurs, stop using it immediately and consult a specialist.

The manual provides no other formal contraindication list.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001-P2-SAFETY-08] FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-MANUAL-001; PDF page 2; panels "Operating recommendations" and "Safety instructions"
