# H₂ Generator Face & Body Exhibition Training Reference

This supplement applies only to `face-body-generator`. Its official user manual remains the higher-priority source for operation and safety.

## Purpose, technology and specifications

The H₂ Generator Face & Body is a portable topical skincare device. It uses ultrasonic atomization at approximately 160 kHz to make a cool ultrafine mist and enriches water with molecular hydrogen. It is not a hot facial steamer. The source lists up to 500 ppb H₂, ORP down to approximately -300 mV, an approximately 55-second mist cycle, a 15 mL reservoir, 1,500 mAh battery, USB-C charging, 1-2 hour charge time, ≤5 W, and dimensions 15.8 × 3.7 × 3 cm.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-SPECS] HK2026 master training PDF, Face & Body sections 1-7 and 12-14.

## Use and care

Fill with low- or medium-mineralization drinking water, close the lid and use the switch to start a roughly 55-second cycle; it may be stopped manually. It is designed for topical misting of face and body. Do not use highly mineralized water, lotions, toners, serums, cosmetic products, essential oils, perfumes or other liquids: they can block or damage the system. Do not tilt beyond 45° during use. For first use, use 35-45°C drinking water for about 30 minutes with the unit off, drain/rinse, fully charge, then refill with fresh drinking water.

Rinse reservoir and lid with clean water. At least monthly, the documented deeper-cleaning procedure uses 3 g citric acid in 30 mL warm drinking water (35-45°C), left for 30 minutes with the device off, then thoroughly rinsed. Do not dishwasher-clean, immerse, use water above 45°C, operate empty or use unsupported fast charging.

Source: [FACE-BODY-GENERATOR-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-CARE] HK2026 master training PDF, Face & Body sections 7-17.

## Boundaries

It is not a room humidifier, Hydrogen Water Bottle, medical nebulizer, inhalation device, water purifier, serum/cosmetic atomizer or medical device. The source discusses skincare-related claims but explicitly says not to independently claim it reverses aging, removes wrinkles, cures acne, treats dermatitis/eczema, eliminates inflammation, replaces sunscreen, repairs UV damage or provides medical treatment. Describe it as a portable hydrogen-enriched skincare mist for face and body hydration only within the supported wording.
