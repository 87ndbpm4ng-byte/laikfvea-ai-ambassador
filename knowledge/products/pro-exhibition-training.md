# Hydrogen Water Bottle PRO Exhibition Training Reference

This supplement applies only to `advanced` (Hydrogen Water Bottle PRO). Its official user manual remains higher priority for operation and safety.

## Product, technology and modes

PRO is a portable hydrogen-water platform with two hydrogen-generation modes (3 and 18 minutes), a 350 mL capacity, optional mineralization, Bluetooth app functions and a dedicated inhalation adapter/tube. It uses controlled electrolysis with a DuPont™ Nafion™ N117 SPE membrane and a platinum-coated titanium electrode. The documented range after completed hydrogen cycles is approximately 2,000-9,000 ppb H₂ and ORP down to approximately -750 mV, depending on source water and conditions. Hydrogen concentration and ORP are distinct; Hydrogen Water and alkaline water are also distinct.

The 3-minute mode is positioned for quicker drinking preparation. The 18-minute mode is for extended generation, higher saturation, inhalation and the mineralization/alkaline option. Do not say either cycle always produces a fixed concentration.

Source: [PRO-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-TECHNOLOGY] HK2026 master training PDF, PRO sections 1 and 4-8.

## Use, inhalation and mineralization

Fill with drinking water to MAX, select 3 or 18 minutes on the touchscreen and start. Before opening after an extended cycle, use the pressure-release valve first. Do not repeatedly run electrolysis on the same water without replacing it. For inhalation, use the supplied adapter and tube with 350 mL drinking water and 18-minute mode; because generated hydrogen exits through the tube, the water display may not reach the maximum possible concentration. Inhalation is a device feature, not medical treatment, oxygen therapy, treatment for disease or medical hydrogen equipment.

For mineralization, place a supplied mineral pouch in the filter cartridge, use about 250-275 mL water, place the cartridge in the bottle and use the recommended 18-minute cycle. Remove and dry the cartridge afterward. The pouch is documented as shungite, silicon, zeolite and magnesium with approximate 3-6 month service life. It can raise pH but the document does not specify a fixed final pH; it is not a complete water-purification system.

## Specifications and smart functions

- Capacity 350 mL / 11.8 fl oz; dimensions 65 × 65 × 210 mm; net weight 440 g; power ≤10 W.
- Main battery 4000 mAh; lid battery 300 mAh; USB-C plus wireless charging up to 5 W.
- Body: Tritan™ plastic and aluminum-alloy housing. Device/electrolyzer life: approximately 8,000 operating hours.
- Touchscreen displays cycle time, estimated ppb, ORP, battery and modes. Documented app functions include pairing, remote electrolysis control, timers, drinking reminders, self-clean/backwash, ppb/ORP and battery monitoring.
- A 280/450 nm lid light function and 7.8 Hz low-frequency module are listed. Do not invent sterilization, neurological, therapeutic or medical claims for either.

Source: [PRO-EXHIBITION-TRAINING-001-SPECS] HK2026 master training PDF, PRO sections 15-21.

## First use, maintenance and limits

Before first drinking use, remove the membrane protection/moisture cap, drain storage liquid, run Self-Cleaning Mode with clean drinking water, drain, and repeat initial cleaning three times. With regular use, self-clean about monthly. Wipe the exterior with a slightly damp cloth; do not dishwasher-clean, rinse the main unit/lid under running water, immerse them, or allow moisture into the charging port. After long storage, use 35-45°C drinking water for about 30 minutes then rinse. Use drinking-quality water only; do not use carbonated, sweet, tea, coffee or other unapproved liquids, and do not generate while charging or use unsupported fast chargers.
