# Water Ionizer User Manual

This approved source applies to the stable product identity `water-ionizer`. The manual covers the Laikfvea and Laikfvea Lite water ionizers. Where the two variants differ, the distinction is stated explicitly.

## Product purpose and operating principle

The manual describes the ionizers as household electrical appliances that use electrolysis to change the pH and oxidation-reduction potential (ORP) of source drinking water. A constant electric current passes through electrodes separated by a membrane. Water and dissolved ions are redistributed into an alkaline cathode fraction and an acidic anode fraction.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P4-5-GENERAL-01] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 4-5; "General Information"

## Water types and production volumes

Depending on mode, one cycle can prepare:

- approximately 3 L of alkaline water together with approximately 0.5 L of acidic water;
- approximately 3 L of acidic water together with approximately 0.5 L of alkaline water;
- approximately 3.5 L of silver water on the Laikfvea model only;
- approximately 3.5 L of hydrogen water.

The Laikfvea Lite model does not have the silver-water function or tilt sensor.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P5-WATER-TYPES-02] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual page 5; model overview and per-cycle volumes

## Package contents and main parts

The manual lists the pitcher, lid, inner cup, removable cup, liquid pH test, membrane, mineral additive, cathode-cleaning cloth, 15 spare membranes plus one installed membrane, and warranty card. A silver electrode is included with the standard model but not Laikfvea Lite.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P6-PACKAGE-03] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual page 6; "Package Contents"

## Preparing for first use

Remove the lid and inner cup with membrane; rinse the pitcher and inner cup; reinstall the cup; fill the inner cup to its maximum mark and pitcher to its minimum mark; connect power; press `START/STOP`; then drain the first prepared alkaline and acidic water.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P7-FIRST-USE-04] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual page 7; "Preparing for First Use"

## Membrane use and replacement

The membrane is 100% natural pressed cotton. Keep it in the inner cup during use. After each use, rinse the inner cup and installed membrane under a gentle stream, air-dry naturally, and store in the pitcher only when completely dry.

With regular use and the recommendations followed, one membrane lasts up to 3 months. Replace it without creasing or twisting, seat it evenly and vertically, and reinstall the removable cup firmly. The manual does not describe a separate drinking-water filter; its documented replaceable component is the separating membrane.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P8-MEMBRANE-05] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual page 8; "Membrane Usage Guidelines"

## Preparing alkaline and acidic water

The selectable ionization level is pH 7.5-11.2. For pH 8.0-9.4, place the inner cup nearer the handle under the anode/dark electrode. For pH 9.5-11.2, place it nearer the spout under the cathode/light electrode; the parallel acidic-water pH appears on the display.

Fill the inner cup to maximum and pitcher between its marks, close the lid, connect power, choose pH with `Up`/`Down`, and press `START/STOP`. The display reports progress and completion, and the selected pH is remembered. Unplug before removing the lid, wipe the cathode, separate the two fractions into glass containers, and dry removable parts.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P9-10-ALKALINE-ACIDIC-06] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 9-10; "Preparing Ionized Alkaline and Acidic Water"

## Hydrogen-water mode

Remove the inner cup, fill the pitcher with drinking water between its marks, close the lid, connect power, and press `START/STOP`. The display reports `Hydrogen water`, progress, then `Hydrogen water ready`. Unplug, wipe the cathode, transfer the water to glass, and dry removable parts.

The manual states that pH remains unchanged and lists molecular hydrogen up to 300 ppb (0.30 ppm) and ORP approximately -200 mV, dependent on source-water composition. These Water Ionizer values are distinct from GO and PRO bottle specifications.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P11-P24-HYDROGEN-08] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 11 and 24

## Silver-water mode limitation

The standard model has a silver-water mode; Lite does not. It uses the silver electrode without the inner cup. Selectable concentration is 0.01-20.0 mg/L and displayed time is 1 second-200 minutes.

The manual conflicts about internal versus external use and contains medical/antimicrobial recommendations. Daniel may describe the mode and controls, but no drinking, medical, hygiene, or bodily application is approved pending review.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P12-13-SILVER-09] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 12-13

## Measuring water pH

Use 5 mL water and 4-5 drops of supplied tester, stir, and compare against the bottle scale. Source water should be pH 6.8-7.6; lower-pH source water may produce inaccurate results. ORP needs a separate meter, which is not included.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P14-PH-TEST-10] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual page 14

## Controls and system messages

Hold `MENU` for several seconds, then use `Up`, `Down`, and `START/STOP`. Settings include Russian/English, Simple/Full menu, hints, voice, melody 1/2/3, volume 25/50/100%, and tilt on/off on the standard model. Messages cover unplugging, sensor errors, open lid, high mineral content, tilt, cup position, missing water, unchanged water, and mode completion.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P15-17-MENU-11] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 15-17

## Source-water requirements

Use drinking-quality water. The technical table specifies mineral content approximately 200-650 ppm and initial temperature +15°C to +45°C. The FAQ gives broader source-specific ranges of at least 50 mg/L and 50-700 mg/L. Because they conflict, approved guidance conservatively uses approximately 200-650 ppm. Do not use distilled water, unmineralized reverse-osmosis water, or boiled water.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P31-P35-SOURCE-WATER-12] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 31 and 35

## Cleaning and maintenance

- Unplug before cleaning.
- Rinse removable water-contact parts under running water no warmer than 40°C and dry thoroughly. Do not dishwasher-clean the unit or submerge the electronic lid.
- Wipe the cathode after use. For buildup, use 9% vinegar or citric-acid solution for 10-15 minutes, then gently wipe, rinse, and dry.
- For heavy buildup, remove the membrane, use 35-40°C water with either 50 g citric acid or 200 mL table vinegar, and leave 6-8 hours before rinsing and drying. Do not connect power while cleaning.
- Do not use brushes or abrasives on the coated anode. Grease may be removed with 96% drinking alcohol or liquid detergent, followed by rinsing and air-drying.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P33-34-CLEANING-13] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 33-34

## Safety instructions

Use indoors with drinking water, within stated temperatures, and keep connectors dry. Do not use a damaged unit, outlet, or cord; operate without water; touch electrodes during operation; submerge the electronic block; modify the unit; allow unsupervised child use; or wash above 40°C, in a dishwasher, or by full immersion.

Source: [WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001-P35-36-SAFETY-14] WATER-IONIZER-MANUAL-001; manual pages 35-36
