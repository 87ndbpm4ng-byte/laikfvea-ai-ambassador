## 1. Water Ionizer — candidate technical content awaiting approval

### Product concept

- Candidate description: a portable countertop system using controlled electrolysis to prepare different water outputs.
- Candidate output categories described in the source: alkaline water, acidic water, hydrogen water, and silver-ion water.
- Candidate distinction: ionization is not filtration, reverse osmosis, desalination, or a substitute for suitable drinking-quality source water.

### Candidate operating and measurement boundaries

- Electrolysis depends on water conductivity and dissolved minerals.
- pH and ORP are different measurements; neither is a direct measure of dissolved molecular hydrogen.
- Source-water composition, mineralization, temperature, selected program, and operating conditions can affect results.
- Hydrogen water and alkaline water must not be presented as the same thing.
- The source describes mineralizer use before ionization of strongly demineralized or reverse-osmosis water. Any exact composition, dilution, or usage instruction requires reconciliation with the approved Water Mineralizer and Water Ionizer manuals.

### Candidate safety and use boundaries

- Do not present the product as a universal water purifier or as making unsafe source water safe.
- Do not infer an approved drinking use for high-pH, acidic, or silver-ion water from this reference.
- Do not make medical, therapeutic, detoxification, blood-alkalization, disease, infection, or fixed-performance claims.
- Do not quote concentrations, pH, ORP, storage periods, cleaning chemistry, membrane life, warranty, service life, temperature limits, or output volumes until each point is confirmed against the approved manual and product approval.
- Do not transfer any silver-ion output to another appliance unless that appliance's approved manual expressly permits it.

## 2. Air Purifier — candidate technical content awaiting approval

### Product concept

- Candidate description: a compact indoor air purifier using a pre-filter, a glass photocatalytic element, and UV-A activation.
- Candidate operating concept: air moves through the device; the pre-filter handles larger dust and the photocatalytic system is intended to act on susceptible contaminants at the active surface.
- Candidate mode labels described in the source: Night, Day, and Boost.

### Candidate boundaries

- Do not present the purifier as a medical device, room sterilizer, universal air-quality solution, replacement for ventilation, humidifier, dehumidifier, professional mold remediation, or guaranteed infection protection.
- Do not claim it destroys every pollutant, microorganism, virus, odor, chemical, or allergen.
- Do not quote performance figures, coverage, CADR, airflow, noise, power, UV emission, laboratory reduction percentages, lifetime, certification, or comparison claims until exact evidence for the exhibited model is approved.
- Do not imply that the photocatalytic element replaces all mechanical particle filtration or that its long-life description means the complete device has no maintenance requirement.
