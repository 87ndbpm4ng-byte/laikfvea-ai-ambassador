## Ambiguities and conflicts

1. The Technical Specifications table permits water up to 176°F (80°C), while Operating Recommendations say water should not exceed 113°F (45°C). First-use instructions also use 95–113°F (35–45°C). Production guidance is conservatively limited to 113°F (45°C) pending manufacturer clarification.
2. The stated container capacity is 13.5 fl oz (400 mL), while the recommended preparation amount is 10 fl oz (300 mL). These are treated as different facts, not a contradiction.
3. The safety instruction about temperatures above 113°F (45°C) or below 32°F (0°C) refers grammatically to device-use temperature; it is not restated as a water-temperature specification.
4. "About 5 minutes" is approximate; it is not represented as an exact cycle duration.

## HEALTH CLAIMS WITHHELD FOR REVIEW

The following manual claims are not included as approved visitor-facing evidence:

- powerful antioxidant that neutralizes free radicals;
- strengthens the immune system;
- improves blood rheology (fluidity) and prevents thrombosis;
- promotes overall body rejuvenation;
- reduces fatigue and increases stress resistance;
- helps normalize metabolism;
- the phrase "prepare healthy water," which implies a health outcome.

The broader promotional implication that these outcomes are benefits of using the product is also withheld. No external evidence was used to evaluate or supplement these claims.
