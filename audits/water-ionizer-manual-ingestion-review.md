## Approved ordinary operational guidance

- electrolysis and separation into alkaline/acidic fractions;
- selectable levels and approximate pH/ORP outputs;
- cup placement, fill levels, controls, progress, completion, separation, unplugging, and drying;
- preparation mechanics for hydrogen and silver modes, without use recommendations;
- source-water, temperature, membrane, cleaning, electrical, and safety requirements;
- pH measurement, menus, and system messages.

## Contradictions and ambiguities

1. Silver water is `internal and external` on page 13, `External use only` on page 17, then given drinking/medical tables on pages 26-27. No use is approved.
2. Alkaline ORP `down to -900 mV` on page 18 conflicts with approximately -750 mV in the detailed technical table on page 36. Production uses the table.
3. Mineral content ~200-650 ppm in the technical table conflicts with FAQ ranges from 50 to 700 mg/L. Production uses ~200-650 ppm.
4. Selectable pH begins at 7.5 on page 9, while detailed outputs begin at 8.0 on page 36. These remain distinct.
5. Manual covers standard and Lite, while exhibition identity does not specify variant. Silver and tilt are always qualified as standard-only.
6. The cotton separator is called a membrane, not a drinking-water filter; no filter system is inferred.
7. Many claims cite external website research not reproduced in the manual; no external source was consulted.
