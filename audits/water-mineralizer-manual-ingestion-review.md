# Water Mineralizer manual ingestion review

Source: `WATER-MINERALIZER-MANUAL-001`, `knowledge/source-materials/water-mineralizer-manual.pdf` (2 PDF pages). Both pages were rendered at high resolution and visually inspected, including headings, composition values, dosage examples, storage conditions, precautions, manufacturer details, and declaration information.

## Source identity and sections

The Russian-language source is titled "Severyanka mineral additive for water - Instructions for use." Sections inspected: Purpose; Composition; Method of use; Storage conditions; Precautions; Shelf life; manufacturer/order details; declaration details.

The source describes a concentrated liquid mineral additive, not an electrical mineralizer device. Production maps it to the exhibition identity `water-mineralizer` while retaining this source-level distinction.

## Approved operational facts

- Intended for addition to purified and/or demineralized drinking water, particularly after deep purification/reverse osmosis.
- Composition: purified water; calcium, magnesium, and potassium ions at the documented concentrate values.
- Recommended dilution 1:1500; 2 mL for an Akvalife jug; about 1/2 teaspoon without the measure; about 1/2 teaspoon per 3 L for ordinary drinking water.
- Mix thoroughly after dosing.
- Prepared water may be used for drinking, cooking, and beverages.
- Storage, sediment/shaking, shelf-life, dilution, undiluted-consumption, and child-access precautions.

## Contradictions and ambiguities

1. The product is named a mineral additive rather than a device, although the exhibition registry calls it Water Mineralizer. Production preserves both identities without inventing hardware behavior.
2. The 1:1500 ratio and the example of 1/2 teaspoon per 3 L are not perfectly reproducible because teaspoon volume is approximate; both are retained exactly as the manual presents them.
3. The Akvalife-jug example gives 2 mL but does not state jug capacity.
4. The source gives concentrate ion values but no final prepared-water mineral concentration, conductivity, or pH.
5. The manual permits slight dosage adjustment based on initial mineralisation and taste and says small deviations do not affect safety, but provides no numeric adjustment bounds.
6. The shelf life is 12 months from manufacture; no separate expiry after opening is provided.
7. A small sediment is expressly allowed, but the manual does not define an unacceptable sediment condition.
