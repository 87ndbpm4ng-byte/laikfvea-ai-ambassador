# Air Purifier manual ingestion review

Source: `AIR-PURIFIER-MANUAL-001`, `knowledge/source-materials/air-purifier-manual.pdf` (25 PDF pages). All pages, diagrams, tables, warnings, and app screenshots were rendered and visually inspected.

## Sections inspected

Contents; package; placement; safety; specifications; getting started; device diagram and touch controls; Night, Day, Boost, Schedule, and automatic-shutoff modes; pre-filter replacement/reset; Capsula Link/app connection and controls; RGB ring; settings; schedule; service/filter resource; cleaning; troubleshooting; manufacturer information.

## Approved operational facts

- Capsula M Size exhibition Air Purifier identity and documented air path.
- Lifelong photocatalytic glass-filter, replaceable pre-filter, and 365 nm ozone-free UV.
- Placement, startup, controls, modes, timer, app, connectivity, filter replacement/reset, maintenance, troubleshooting, safety, and technical specifications.
- Optional sensors remain explicitly optional and are not presented as standard equipment.

## Pollutant and performance claim audit

The manual gives no explicit pollutant list or filtration/removal percentage. It does not document dust, PM2.5, PM10, pollen, smoke, odors, VOCs, formaldehyde, pet dander, bacteria, viruses, mold, pathogens, HEPA efficiency, CADR, or airflow rate. The diagram labels incoming air as dirty and outgoing air as clean, but no tested removal claim or health outcome is inferred.

## HEALTH / MEDICAL / MARKETING CLAIMS WITHHELD

The supplied manual contains no explicit disease-prevention, therapeutic, asthma, allergy, infection, immune, sleep, wellness, antimicrobial, pathogen-removal, virus-killing, or bacteria-killing claims. It also supplies no pollutant-removal percentages. Accordingly, none were introduced from external or general knowledge.

The labels `dirty air` and `clean air`, and mode descriptions such as `optimal cleaning` or `best efficiency`, are treated as qualitative manual/interface wording, not verified pollutant or health performance claims.

## Contradictions and ambiguities

1. The document is a mixed-language draft with placeholders such as illustration notes, figure `N`, unfinished component wording, `service life ???`, and an incomplete certification section.
2. The manual says `Capsula app` and `Capsula Link app`; these appear to refer to the same control app, but naming is inconsistent.
3. Optional sensors are listed as `available from mid 2022`; the manual does not establish whether the exhibition unit contains them. Production always qualifies them as optional.
4. Coverage `up to 218 sq. ft. (20.25 m²)` has no CADR, room-height, air-change, mode, or test-condition basis. It is not extrapolated.
5. `Glass-filter: Lifelong` gives no numerical life or conditions; overall device service life is explicitly unresolved (`???`).
6. UV is described as 365 nm and ozone-free, but the manual does not quantify ozone or document a test method.
7. The diagram and text establish a pre-filter, photocatalytic glass-filter, UV LEDs, and fan behavior, but do not provide a detailed purification reaction/mechanism. No additional mechanism is inferred.
8. Dimensions are printed as two measurements for a cylindrical product and labeled `6.57 in (17.7 cm) x 10.12 in (25.75 cm)`; the inch/cm conversions do not exactly correspond. Both printed forms are preserved without correction.
9. Page 2 lists an adapter as optional while page 5 gives 110-240 V input/power-converter details; no specific plug/adapter inclusion is promised.

## Retrieval and isolation

Routing uses only the explicit product identities `Air Purifier` and `Capsula M Size`, plus localized equivalents. Generic `air`, `clean`, `machine`, `device`, `filter`, and `purifier` are not introduced as independent product aliases. The source remains isolated from GO, PRO, Water Ionizer, Face & Body Generator, and pending Water Mineralizer evidence.

Unsupported CADR, HEPA, pollutant-removal percentage, medical-benefit, and standard-sensor questions remain outside approved evidence.
