# Final Product-Information Knowledge Gap Audit

## 1. Executive summary

The six-product knowledge base is technically mature but not yet complete as an exhibition conversation layer. Daniel and Emily can reliably explain documented identity, operation, specifications, maintenance, safety, and product-specific capabilities from the six approved manuals. Product isolation is strong: GO and PRO remain distinct, PRO inhalation remains PRO-specific, the standalone Water Mineralizer remains separate from PRO mineralisation, and the Air Purifier remains isolated from water products.

The principal remaining gap is not technical documentation. It is **approved visitor interpretation**. The manuals often explain how a product works but do not safely answer natural exhibition questions such as:

- Why would I use this?
- What are its main advantages?
- What makes it different from an ordinary alternative?
- Who is it for?
- What practical result should I expect?
- Which product should I choose for my situation?

Those answers require approved product-positioning, intended-use, expected-effect, and comparison statements. Specifications must not be relabelled as advantages, and withheld health, medical, cosmetic, antimicrobial, or performance claims must not be used to close the gap.

- expected noticeable results that do not become health/cosmetic claims;
- competitor/category differentiation;
- recommended frequency or practical routines where manuals are silent;
- cross-product recommendations beyond documented capabilities;
- a safe, approved stand demonstration narrative.

## 10. Advantages and positioning gaps

### Classification rule

1. **APPROVED FACT** — direct manual statement, with qualifiers.
2. **SUPPORTABLE PRODUCT FEATURE** — a documented capability that can be described neutrally, but not called superior.
3. **POSITIONING CLAIM REQUIRING MANAGEMENT APPROVAL** — intended visitor/user value or selection rationale.
4. **COMPETITIVE CLAIM REQUIRING EVIDENCE** — comparison with alternatives, market norms or competitors.
5. **HEALTH/MEDICAL CLAIM — WITHHOLD** — physiological, therapeutic, cosmetic or pathogen/health outcome.

## 13. Commercial handoff boundary

### Intended behavior

Pricing, MOQ, OEM, Private Label, distribution, samples, purchasing, payment, shipping and negotiated warranty/commitment questions should receive a short localized response equivalent to:

> For pricing, MOQ, OEM or distribution enquiries, please speak with a member of our team at the stand.

### Current behavior

The current system is safe in that exact pricing and other factual details cannot be invented without approved knowledge. However, it is **not yet deterministic enough for the corrected scope**:

- `BUYING_INTEREST` is broad rather than a closed commercial-handoff intent;
- response strategy still contains commercial/recommendation concepts;
- the system prompt forbids unsupported pricing but normally produces the generic approved-information boundary;
- no shared five-language fixed handoff rule was found for all listed commercial categories.

### Smallest future change

Add one narrow, deterministic, multilingual `COMMERCIAL_HANDOFF` boundary before factual retrieval/generation:

1. recognize explicit price, MOQ, OEM, Private Label, distribution, sample, purchase, payment, shipping and negotiated commercial-term intent;
2. return one localized visitor-friendly handoff string;
3. preserve the current product context but collect no data;
4. perform no commercial retrieval and make no commitment;
5. add five-language tests and false-positive tests for product warranty/maintenance questions.

No commercial database, CRM, lead state or personal-data collection is required.
