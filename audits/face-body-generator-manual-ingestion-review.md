- Identity: portable hydrogen skin humidifier; exhibition identity Hydrogen Water Generator for Face & Body.

- Components: lid, 15 mL reservoir, spray lens/nozzle, switch, main body.

## Manufacturer restrictions and safety

- Low- or medium-mineral drinking water only; no highly mineralized water, lotions, or cosmetics.
- No empty-reservoir operation, water above 45°C, immersion, dishwasher, fast charging, or subzero exposure.
- Keep charging port/cable dry; use supplied cable; recommended adapter 2A/5V.
- Maximum 45-degree tilt during operation; transport with water only vertically.
- Stop immediately and consult a specialist if irritation or another adverse reaction occurs.
- No other formal contraindications are listed.

## Contradictions and ambiguities

1. The product is called a hydrogen-water generator in branding/exhibition identity but a portable hydrogen skin humidifier in the manual; production retains the registry identity while accurately describing the manual's spray function.
2. `160 kHz/s` is an unusual printed unit for vibration frequency. It is preserved verbatim rather than corrected.
3. Hydrogen `from 500 ppb` and ORP `from -300 mV` do not specify test conditions, tolerance, nominal/maximum status, or precise directional interpretation. They are not restated as guaranteed fixed values.
4. The manual says the ultrasonic mist is saturated with molecular hydrogen but does not document the hydrogen-generation mechanism beyond that statement.
5. The manual refers to face/body positioning through its product identity but gives no body-area procedure, distance, application duration beyond the 55-second cycle, or usage frequency.
6. No weight, battery runtime, service life, or formal contraindication list is supplied.

## Retrieval and isolation

Retrieval uses the stable `face-body-generator` product identity and narrow full-name/portable-hydrogen-skin-sprayer aliases.
