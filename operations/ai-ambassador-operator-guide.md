# AI Ambassador — Exhibition Operator Guide

## What the system is

Daniel and Emily are product-information assistants for the six exhibition products. They support English, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Cantonese, and French.

Visitors can speak, type, choose a Quick Question, or browse products. Pricing, MOQ, OEM, purchasing, samples, shipping, and other commercial questions are deliberately handed to stand staff. The AI does not replace staff for commercial discussions.

## Morning startup

1. Power on the tablet, display, and approved audio equipment.
2. Confirm the device is connected to the approved internet connection.
3. Open the approved production URL: `______________________________`.
4. Enter the approved kiosk or full-screen browser mode where appropriate.
5. Confirm the language-selection screen is visible.
6. Check that the speaker volume is audible but suitable for the stand environment.
7. Confirm the browser’s microphone permission state is appropriate. Do not request the microphone until a visitor presses **Talk**.
8. Select a language and Daniel or Emily; confirm the visual panel says **Ready when you are**.
9. Ask one short typed product question and confirm a readable answer appears.
10. If approved for that day, perform one short **Talk** test and confirm listening, processing, and recovery behavior.
11. Confirm **End Conversation** returns the kiosk to language selection.
12. End the test conversation and leave the kiosk on the language screen before visitors arrive.

**FINAL DEVICE CONFIRMATION REQUIRED:** exact power sequence, kiosk/browser mode, audio route, screen-sleep setting, approved URL, and network procedure depend on the final hardware.

## Normal visitor flow

Language → Daniel or Emily → question or Product Explorer → answer → follow-up → **End Conversation** → next visitor.

Visitors may type, use **Talk**, select a Quick Question, or browse the Product Explorer. Staff may help only when a visitor is unsure how to start or needs a commercial discussion.

### Visual specialist states

| State | What it means | Staff action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ready when you are** | The assistant is ready; no visual conversation has started yet. | Normal. Let the visitor ask a question. |
| **Preparing Daniel/Emily** | The visual specialist is being prepared after a real interaction. | Normal. Allow a short delay. |
| **Visual specialist available** | Visual conversation is ready. | No action. |
| **Visual unavailable** | The image could not be prepared. Text conversation remains usable. | Encourage typing, Talk if available, or Quick Questions. |

## What staff should not worry about

These can be normal:

- No specialist image before the visitor’s first real interaction.
- A short preparing delay after a visitor asks a question.
- A visitor choosing typing instead of Talk.
- A readable text answer remaining on screen if audio does not play.
- A commercial question being redirected to a staff member.

## When staff should intervene

Step in when:

- the visitor needs price, MOQ, OEM, Private Label, ordering, samples, payment, shipping, distribution, or other commercial commitment;
- the visitor repeatedly requests unsupported medical or therapeutic claims;
- the device is physically unresponsive;
- the visitor cannot continue after one reasonable retry;
- the network has been unavailable for an extended period;
- a visitor needs a product demonstration or conversation beyond approved product information.

## End Conversation and next visitor

Use **End Conversation** when a visitor has finished or walked away and the automatic reset has not yet occurred. It clears the conversation, product context, listening/speaking state, and prepares the kiosk for the next visitor.

Before welcoming a new visitor, check that the language-selection screen is shown and that no previous answer, product name, or audio remains.

## Inactivity reset

- After **2 minutes** without activity, the kiosk displays a warning for the final **15 seconds**.
- The visitor may select **Continue session**.
- Otherwise, the kiosk returns to language selection automatically.
- The inactivity timer pauses while the assistant is listening, processing, or speaking.

## Privacy

- Do not ask visitors to enter unnecessary personal information.
- The AI is not intended for lead collection or contact storage.
- Use **End Conversation** after a visitor where appropriate.
- Previous visitor content should not remain visible. If it does, stop using the kiosk and follow the recovery card.

## End-of-day procedure

1. Press **End Conversation** and confirm the language screen is visible.
2. Confirm no speech, listening indicator, or prior visitor content remains.
3. Close or lock the kiosk/browser using the approved device procedure.
4. Connect the device to power if required.
5. Record unresolved issues, time observed, and what was happening when they occurred.
6. Tell the next operator or stand manager about any issue that needs follow-up.
